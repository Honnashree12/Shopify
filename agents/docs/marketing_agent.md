# Marketing Agent (`marketing_agent.py`)

## 📌 Overview
The **Marketing Agent** (`marketing_agent`) is a specialized E-Commerce Copywriter and Growth Marketer agent responsible for expanding high-level marketing strategy into multi-channel promotional copy.

---

## 🎯 Primary Purpose
- Transforms product details and campaign strategy into promotional assets.
- Generates channel-tailored copy for Social Media (Instagram, Facebook), Direct Messaging (WhatsApp), and Direct Email campaigns.
- Writes 3 punchy benefit taglines for landing pages and product badges.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Exposed & Used
- **`get_product_context()`**: Reads product metadata.
- **`save_marketing_assets(...)`**: Stores marketing assets dictionary into `tool_context.state["marketing_assets"]`.

---

## 📣 Generated Copy Assets
1. **Campaign Headline**: High-conversion title (e.g. *"This Independence Day, Look Beyond The Sky"*).
2. **Short Campaign Description**: 2-sentence quick summary.
3. **Long Marketing Copy**: Emotionally engaging storytelling narrative connecting STEM learning and curiosity.
4. **Social Media Caption**: Standard Facebook/LinkedIn copy with hashtags.
5. **Instagram Caption**: Emoji-friendly, visually formatted caption.
6. **WhatsApp Message**: Direct messaging template with bold formatting and CTA link.
7. **Email Subject**: High open-rate subject line.
8. **Email Body**: Complete HTML/text promotional email body.
9. **Call To Action (CTA)**: Action-oriented checkout prompt.
10. **Taglines**: 3 bullet points summarizing core value props.
